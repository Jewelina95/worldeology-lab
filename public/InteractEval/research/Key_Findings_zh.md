# InteractNet · Key Findings

1. [今天模型间真正拉开差距的，是主动推进交互的能力。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=stage-profile&finding=F01)
2. [更多信息反而可能让模型表现更差。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=information&finding=F07)
3. [较强的基础执行能力，并不能避免模型在信息缺失时编造事实。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=gap-correlation&finding=F09)
4. [单轮表现出色，不代表整段交互能够成功。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=case-trajectories&finding=F13)
5. [更换交互对象，可能逆转 AI 模型之间的能力排名。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=pairings&finding=F19)
6. [自我交互评测可能使不同角色下行为模式相似的模型获得评分优势。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=self-gap&finding=F22)
7. [随着交互参与人数增加，AI更容易失去主动协调能力，而非基础响应能力。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=group-size&finding=F28)
8. [认知架构不仅能增强AI，还可能改变它的交互能力优势。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=architecture-purpose&finding=F26)
9. [认知架构对较弱模型的主动能力提升更大，但高水平前沿贡献仍集中在较强模型上。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=architecture-headroom&finding=F30)
10. [AI的内部表征可以编码好感信息，却未必能可靠区分好感属于哪一方。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=shared-affection&finding=F32)
11. [读懂AI的内部状态，不代表就能操控它的行为。](https://worldeology.com/InteractNet/index.html#comparisons?measure=representation-control&finding=F35)
