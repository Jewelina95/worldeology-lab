主要发现与时序图，中英文快速版。总体数值来自论文表格；时序图展示论文记录中的具体对话，不参与排行榜计算。

01

真正拉开差距的是主动推进，而不只是接住上一句话。

这十个模型在被动执行上的分差只有 0.035，到了主动适应扩大为 0.370，约为 10.6 倍。同样做到保留目标、吸收信息，并不意味着同样能提出方向、持续推进。

Initiative separates systems more than reliable replies do.

Across these ten models, the score range grows from 0.035 in passive execution to 0.370 in active adaptation—about 10.6×. Preserving goals and taking up information do not imply the same ability to initiate and sustain a direction.

02

每一轮都能过关，对话仍可能越聊越没进展。

两段同场景、同标准用户的记录，前两轮六级评分完全一致，后续走向却分开：一段兴趣从 0.60 降到 0.20 后结束，另一段升到 0.78 并成功收束。单轮得分、交互走向和结束原因需要放在同一时间轴上看。

Acceptable turns can still add up to a stalled conversation.

Two episodes with the same scene and standard user have identical six-level grades for their first two turns. Their courses diverge: interest falls from 0.60 to 0.20 in one, but rises to 0.78 with successful closure in the other. Turn scores, trajectory, and ending need a shared time axis.

03

信息半缺半错，有时比明确缺信息更容易诱发编造。

在论文的信息条件分组中，补入规格外事实的比例在 partial 条件达到 16.4%，高于 insufficient 的 10.3%；后者的澄清率反而更高，为 37.4%。partial 混合了信息不全与错误前提，两者应进一步拆开分析。

A partly specified or misleading brief can invite more fabrication than an explicit gap.

Fabrication reaches 16.4% in the partial-information condition, above 10.3% with insufficient information. The latter elicits more clarification, at 37.4%. The partial condition combines incomplete briefs with false premises; those conditions warrant separate analysis.

04

“成功结束”取决于场景目的，不能直接代替能力。

以 claude-opus-5.5 为例，任务型场景的成功结束率为 23.1%，开放式场景为 3.8%。陪伴或关系型对话本来就未必以完成任务为终点，因此应在相同目的下比较结束率。

Successful closure depends on what the interaction is for.

For claude-opus-5.5, successful closure is 23.1% in task-oriented settings and 3.8% in open-ended settings. Companionship and relational exchanges need not end by completing a task, so closure rates should be compared within the same purpose.

05

分数属于一段搭配，不完全属于模型本身。

换成自我对话后，gpt-6-astra 的得分由标准用户条件下的 0.629 变为 0.677，claude-opus-5.5 则由 0.636 变为 0.599。对手方一变，两者的相对次序也会改变。

A score belongs partly to the pairing.

Under self-play, gpt-6-astra scores 0.677 rather than 0.629 against the standard user, while claude-opus-5.5 scores 0.599 rather than 0.636. Changing the partner reverses their relative ordering.

06

架构带来的增益，最集中在主动适应。

MetaMind、Cognizer、Claude Code 的平均增益都在主动阶段最大。Cognizer 的主动阶段增益为 +0.066，被动阶段为 +0.012；这提示需要看能力剖面，才能看清架构改善了哪里。

Architecture gains concentrate at the active end.

MetaMind, Cognizer, and Claude Code all show their largest mean stage gain in active adaptation. Cognizer gains +0.066 there versus +0.012 in passive execution. The capability profile shows where an architecture makes a difference.
